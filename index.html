Working principles

The analysis should survive scrutiny.

  1. 01

    Start with the engineering question.

    Methods are chosen for the decision they support, not for novelty alone.

  2. 02

    Build validation into the workflow.

    Preprocessing and feature selection are learned inside validation folds.

  3. 03

    Separate evidence from mechanism.

    Statistical association generates process questions; it does not prove root cause.

  4. 04

    Make the work reproducible.

    Notebooks, decisions, model evaluations, figures, and reports remain open for review.
